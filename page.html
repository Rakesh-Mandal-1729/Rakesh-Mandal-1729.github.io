Research

TopoFlow

Under review · CMAME

One conditional flow-matching model for topology optimisation in both 2D and 3D. A U-Net velocity field, conditioned through Fourier features and FiLM, turns noise into structures for a given load and material budget, with no architectural changes between dimensions. Every design is checked by finite-element analysis.

1.008median compliance ratio vs. classical SIMP (2D)
25–100×lower cost than SIMP (2D)
96.5%of 3D designs within 10% of the FEM reference

RobustFlow

In preparation · SAMO

A two-stage architecture for robust design optimisation. A flow-matching prior learns feasible designs, then robust fine-tuning pushes the whole generated population towards designs that hold up under manufacturing variation, using either a differentiable solver or a learned surrogate as the physics critic.

−67%mean compliance (2D)
18.8×lower spread in compliance
904industrial wheel CAD models learned in 3D
<0.4%variation in eigenfrequency across generated wheels

FM-DUF

In preparation

Design under fabrication uncertainty. A hierarchical flow-matching model learns nominal shapes and how they deform when made; a wavelet neural operator predicts performance, and Bayesian optimisation picks shapes whose post-fabrication performance is best at a chosen quantile. Shown on airfoils and optical metasurface absorbers.

What's next

Objective 1Reliability-aware design

Move from mean-and-variance objectives to explicit limits on the probability of failure, and unify robust and reliability-based design.

Objective 2Efficient generation

Cut the compute and energy per design with few-step flows and energy-efficient, including spiking, generative models.

Objective 3Multimodal design

Let engineers state requirements in language and sketches, with a human in the loop and hard constraints kept intact.

Objective 4Industrial applications

Take the framework from benchmarks to production-scale components, with finite-element verification of every design.
